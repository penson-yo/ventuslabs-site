The foundation

Built on the world’s most popular AI agent framework.

OpenClaw is one of the most-starred projects on GitHub. It’s a self-hosted, open-source agentic framework that connects to any LLM and runs on your own infrastructure. Your data stays yours. Your agent runs 24/7. No subscriptions, no lock-in.

How it works

We build it. You own it.

  1. 01

    We scope your agent

    We work out which use cases matter most to you, which platforms you want to use, and what your agent needs to know about your life to be genuinely useful.

  2. 02

    We configure and deploy

    We set up the OpenClaw instance, connect your chosen LLM backend (Claude, GPT, DeepSeek), wire up your messaging platforms, and configure the memory and tools your agent needs.

  3. 03

    Your agent runs on your infrastructure

    Everything lives on your own server. Your data never touches a third-party platform. The agent is always on, always learning, and entirely yours.
